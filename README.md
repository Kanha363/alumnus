# 🎓 Alumnus Hub

**Alumnus Hub** is a centralized web platform designed to connect **students, alumni, and the institution** in one place. It helps institutions manage alumni information while providing alumni with opportunities for networking, mentorship, events, jobs, and community engagement.

## 🚀 Features

* 👤 **Alumni Registration & Profiles** — Alumni can create and manage their profiles.
* 🔍 **Alumni Directory** — Search and explore alumni based on relevant information.
* 🎓 **Student–Alumni Networking** — Connect students with alumni for guidance and interaction.
* 🤝 **Mentorship** — Alumni can support students through mentorship and career guidance.
* 📅 **Events & Reunions** — Create and manage alumni events and reunions.
* 💼 **Jobs & Opportunities** — Share job openings, internships, and career opportunities.
* 💬 **Communication** — Enable interaction between students and alumni.
* 💰 **Donations & Contributions** — Support institutional initiatives through alumni contributions.
* 🔐 **Authentication & Verification** — Secure user authentication and alumni verification.
* 📊 **Centralized Alumni Data** — Maintain alumni information in an organized database.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React.js, Tailwind CSS
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
* **Database:** PostgreSQL
* **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication
* **Deployment/Cloud:** AWS

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of Alumnus Hub is to create a **centralized digital ecosystem** where alumni can stay connected with their institution, interact with students, participate in events, share opportunities, and contribute to the growth of their institution.
